# Heritage Builder — Game Play

## Level Structure

| Level | Name / Setting | Theme | Core Gameplay Focus | Narrative Goal | Player Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The River’s Gift (Lowland Rice Plains) | Foundation & Survival | Learn farming, housing, irrigation | Prove your discipline by turning floods into fertile land | Harmony with nature brings stability |
| 2 | The Mountain Gate (Highland Pass) | Expansion & Trade | Resource extraction, trade routes, transport | Secure trade for the kingdom | Prosperity grows from connection |
| 3 | The Coastal Haven (Fishing Port City) | Prosperity & Diplomacy | Cultural satisfaction, exports, foreign trade | Balance wealth with cultural identity | Growth must not erase tradition |
| 4 | The Frontier Citadel (Borderlands) | Defense & Governance | City defense, morale, taxation, political tension | Maintain order under pressure | Leadership means loyalty and fairness |
| 5 | The Crown of Merit (Central Capital Region) | Legacy & Ascension | Advanced systems integration, moral choices | Build the new capital and prove you’re worthy to rule | True kingship is earned through virtue |

## Gameplay Progression

### Level 1 — The River’s Gift (Lowland Rice Plains)

- **Region:** Northern Vietnam - Red River Delta (Inspired by Thăng Long outskirts / Bắc Ninh)
- **Music:** đàn bầu
- **Emotional tone:** Hopeful, learning

**The River’s Gift:** Tutorial level; teaches basic city-building systems.

**Key mechanics:** food production, irrigation management, flood control, happiness.

**Story:** You reclaim abandoned floodplains to feed the realm.

**Win condition:** Survive 3 flood seasons and maintain population growth.

### Level 2 — The Mountain Gate (Highland Pass)

- **Region:** Hòa Bình Highlands (Gateway to the Northwest)
- **Music:** gong ensembles
- **Emotional tone:** Determined, ambitious.

**The Mountain Gate:** Expands player control to logistics and resource economy.

**Introduces:** quarrying, mining, woodcutting, trade posts, caravans.

**Story:** Build a trade hub linking northern provinces.

**Win condition:** Establish profitable trade and sustain city stability for 2 years.

### Level 3 — The Coastal Haven (Fishing Port City)

- **Region:** Hội An (Old Cham Coast, later Vietnamese trade port), Central Vietnam
- **Music:** gong ensembles
- **Emotional tone:** Confident, reflective.

**The Coastal Haven:** Expands to diplomacy, aesthetics, and luxury production.

**Introduces:** exports (ceramics, salt, fish), cultural satisfaction, festivals.

**Story:** Foreign merchants arrive — you must grow wealth without losing virtue.

**Win condition:** Reach prosperity and cultural balance simultaneously.

### Level 4 — The Frontier Citadel (Borderlands)

- **Region:** Tây Sơn Highlands (Bình Định / Gia Lai area)
- **Music:** gong ensembles
- **Emotional tone:** Dark, tense, moral.

**The Frontier Citadel:** Introduces governance and moral systems.

**Mechanics:** taxes, decrees, loyalty meter, minor rebellions.

**Story:** Protect borderlands and show wisdom under unrest.

**Win condition:** Keep stability and loyalty above thresholds through political crises.

### Level 5 — The Crown of Merit (Central Capital Region)

- **Region:** Huế (Imperial Capital Region)
- **Music:** court music
- **Emotional tone:** Epic, emotional fulfillment.

**The Crown of Merit:** Final challenge combining all systems.

**Introduces:** moral decisions, Mandate Meter, imperial projects (palace, roads, shrines).

**Story:** Emperor’s death triggers judgment — your cities determine your legitimacy.

**Win condition:** Achieve “Mandate of Heaven” rating through prosperity, harmony, and virtue.

**Outcome:** You ascend as king, completing the demo arc. -> “The people remember your walls not for their height - but for the peace they shelter.”
