# Heritage Builder — Campaigns and Missions

There are 30 missions in three campaigns, played in order: Đại Long, then Kambara, then Tampadesa. Missions 1–5 of Campaign I teach the game. Every other mission is a full city-building challenge that moves the story on. The story is in [story.md](story.md). The systems, goal types and mission families are in the [GDD](gdd.md#7-missions-and-win-conditions).

**About the numbers:** every target below is a placeholder for balancing. What matters at this stage is each mission's type, what it teaches or introduces, and its story beat.

**Mission types:** **M** = metrics, **H** = heritage monument, **S** = survival.

## Campaign I — The Dragon Legacy (Đại Long)

| # | Mission | Setting | WY | Type | New systems |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The River's Gift | Delta flood plain | 1–3 | M | Roads, housing, wells, rice paddies, granary, market, workers |
| 2 | Fish and Clay | Coastal delta | 3–6 | M | Fishing, clay and pottery, storage yard, taxes and treasury |
| 3 | The Village of Spirits | Karst valley | 6–9 | M | Shrines and temples, communal hall, health, fire and collapse, festivals, Culture rating |
| 4 | Taming the Cinnabar River | Delta lowland | 9–15 | M | Seasons, the flood cycle, dykes and sluices, irrigation, patron favour, disease |
| 5 | The Mountain Market | Highland pass | 15–20 | M | Timber and mining, trade post, the world map, trade routes, court requests, the Mandate rating |
| 6 | The Southern Shore | The port of Hội Thủy | 20–24 | M + S | Docks and sea trade, the silk chain, elite housing, first defences: palisade, watchtower, garrison |
| 7 | The Scholar's Gate | Provincial city | 24–28 | H | Academy and examinations, scholar-officials, monuments and guilds |
| 8 | The Western Marches | Border highlands | 28–32 | S + M | Walls, gatehouses, towers, fortress, weapons, the levy choice |
| 9 | The River of Stakes | Estuary | 32–34 | S | River stakes, naval attacks, moats, gifts and tribute |
| 10 | The Ascending Dragon | Thăng Hà, the new capital | 34–36 | H + M | Everything together: the Imperial Citadel |

### 1. The River's Gift

- **Setting:** an abandoned flood plain on the Sông Son. A small map with no threats. The start is empty apart from a road entry.
- **Teaches:**
  - camera and roads;
  - placing houses, and settlers moving in;
  - wells;
  - rice paddies, the granary and the market;
  - jobs and workers;
  - how a house upgrades.
- **Goals:** population 150; 10 houses at level 1.
- **Story:** Khai Sơn arrives with a few dozen settlers. Master Văn Lễ: *"A city begins with a full granary, not a wall."*

### 2. Fish and Clay

- **Setting:** a coastal village where the river meets the sea.
- **Teaches:**
  - the fishing wharf;
  - the clay pit and the pottery kiln;
  - the storage yard;
  - the tax office, the treasury and wages;
  - level 2 housing, which needs pottery.
- **Goals:** population 300; treasury 1,500; 15 houses at level 2.
- **Story:** the first tax caravan is sent to the capital. The court notices.

### 3. The Village of Spirits

- **Setting:** a valley among karst towers, with existing villages to win over.
- **Teaches:**
  - the shrine, the temple and the village communal hall;
  - the apothecary;
  - the fire watch and the builders' guild (fire and collapse risk);
  - the first festival;
  - the Culture rating and the ratings panel.
- **Goals:** population 500; Culture 25; one festival held.
- **Story:** the villagers trust their guardian spirits more than a stranger from court. Khai Sơn honours their communal hall before he builds his own office.

### 4. Taming the Cinnabar River

- **Setting:** the low delta, rich and dangerous.
- **Teaches:**
  - wet and dry seasons;
  - the flood cycle and floodplain fertility;
  - dykes and sluice gates, and dyke breaches;
  - irrigation and a second harvest;
  - the Mountain Lord and the Water Lord, and patron favour;
  - disease after a flood.
- **Goals:** survive 3 flood seasons without a breach; population 800; Prosperity 30.
- **Story:** the Water Lord's anger and the Mountain Lord's patience. This is the mission where the river becomes the player's ally.

### 5. The Mountain Market

- **Setting:** a highland pass linking the delta and the hill clans.
- **Teaches:**
  - lumberyards and mines;
  - the trade post and the world map;
  - opening a route and setting buy and sell limits;
  - court requests;
  - the Mandate (Legitimacy) rating.
- **Goals:** trade income 1,000 a year; fulfil 3 court requests; Mandate 30.
- **Story:** the Emperor names Khai Sơn Governor of the Southern Provinces. Prince Long Uy is seen for the first time, cold at the ceremony. *The tutorial ends here.*

### 6. The Southern Shore

- **Setting:** the port of Hội Thủy, with foreign merchants from Vijara, Hamsara and the far traders.
- **Introduces:**
  - the dock and sea trade;
  - the mulberry and silk chain;
  - elite housing;
  - the first defences: palisade, watchtower and garrison with automatic militia.
- **Threats:** Vijara pirates raid twice.
- **Goals:** Prosperity 40; exports worth 1,500 a year; 4 elite houses; survive both raids.
- **Story:** Ngọc Lan joins Khai Sơn. The House of Ja of Vijara: traders by day, while their cousins raid by night.

### 7. The Scholar's Gate

- **Setting:** a provincial city to be made a centre of learning.
- **Introduces:**
  - the academy, examinations and scholar-officials who staff the tax offices and courts;
  - heritage monuments and guilds (carpenters, masons).
- **Monument:** **the Temple of Learning**, in 4 stages: gate, courtyards, hall, stele garden.
- **Goals:** the Temple of Learning complete; Culture 50.
- **Story:** the Emperor falls ill. As the last stage completes, word arrives that he has died.

### 8. The Western Marches

- **Setting:** the border highlands towards Kambara.
- **Introduces:**
  - walls and gatehouses;
  - towers and the fortress;
  - the weapons workshop;
  - earthen ramparts;
  - **the levy choice:** a scripted request to pay a harsh levy, either from the treasury or by raising taxes on the people.
- **Threats:** 4 waves of Kambara border raids.
- **Goals:** survive all waves; Mandate 40; population 1,500.
- **Story:** King Long Uy's first decree. Khai Sơn defends, will not strike back, and makes a truce with Kambara's envoy, Lady Sovanna. This is the same war as Campaign II, mission 4.

### 9. The River of Stakes

- **Setting:** an estuary city guarding the river route to the heartland.
- **Introduces:**
  - river stakes;
  - naval attacks;
  - moats;
  - archer militia;
  - gifts and tribute to avert attacks.
- **Threats:** the Northern Throne invades by land and river, with escalating waves.
- **Goals:** hold until the end of WY 34 with the palace intact. Optional distinction: no house burned.
- **Story:** the King flees the old capital, and Khai Sơn holds the river. A comic follows: the Silent Siege and Long Uy's abdication.

### 10. The Ascending Dragon

- **Setting:** Thăng Hà, where the capital moves. The map is large, with a river, a lake and karst.
- **Introduces:** nothing new. This is the campaign's final exam.
- **Monument:** **the Imperial Citadel**, in 5 stages: walls and moat, the gates, the flag tower, the throne hall, the gardens.
- **Goals:** the Citadel complete; Mandate 70; Prosperity 60; population 4,000.
- **Story:** Khai Sơn's edict on moving the capital. The stele is finished, and Campaign II unlocks.

## Campaign II — The Temple Mountain (Kambara)

| # | Mission | Setting | WY | Type | New systems |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Breathing Lake | Lakeshore stilt town | 22–24 | M | Kambara buildings, stilt housing, the lake's pulse, floating rice, the Divine Favour rating |
| 2 | The Western Baray | The plain west of the capital | 24–27 | M | Barays, canals, dry-season irrigation |
| 3 | Stones of the Holy Hills | Quarry town | 27–29 | M | Sandstone and laterite quarries, elephant labour, the stone barge quay, temple estates |
| 4 | The Royal Road | The eastern march | 29–32 | S + M | Royal roads and rest houses, laterite walls, moats, elephant militia, gifts and truces |
| 5 | Suryagiri | The capital's outskirts | 32–38 | H | A great staged monument, sculptors, temple dance |
| 6 | Tribute of the Sea | The coastal frontier | 40–55 | M + S | Heavy court tribute, a poor start, storms, sea trade with Vijara |
| 7 | The Burning of Yasonagara | A lakeside city | 60–62 | S | Large naval invasion, refugee waves |
| 8 | The Exile's Return | A road city | 62–66 | M | Royal hospitals, the shift in faith, incoming aid |
| 9 | The Great City | Yasonagara rebuilt | 66–72 | S + H | A walled and moated city with gate towers as a monument, the final assault |
| 10 | The Temple of Four Faces | The heart of Yasonagara | 72–80 | H + M | The great monument, peace with Vijara |

### 1. The Breathing Lake

- **Setting:** a stilt town on the shore of Tonlé Thom.
- **Introduces:**
  - Kambara's buildings and goods;
  - stilt housing, which survives high water;
  - **the lake's pulse:** the shoreline moves with the season, and fishing grounds and floating-rice fields move with it;
  - **Divine Favour.**
- **Goals:** population 1,000; Divine Favour 30.
- **Story:** King Suryadeva gives Sovanna the lake provinces. Achar Vama: *"The lake breathes. Build so the city breathes with it."*

### 2. The Western Baray

- **Setting:** a dry plain west of the capital.
- **Introduces:**
  - the baray, a reservoir that fills in the wet season;
  - canals that feed fields, moats and houses through the dry season;
  - a water network on the overlay.
- **Goals:** 20 fields irrigated through a full dry season; 3,000 rice in storage; population 1,800.
- **Story:** Sovanna's family trade becomes the realm's lifeline.

### 3. Stones of the Holy Hills

- **Setting:** a quarry town at the foot of the sacred sandstone hills.
- **Introduces:**
  - sandstone and laterite quarries;
  - the elephant stable, which supplies labour that hauls stone;
  - the stone barge quay;
  - **temple estates**, temples that hold farms and workers.
- **Goals:** fulfil 6 stone requests from the capital; trade income 1,500 a year.
- **Story:** the stone for Suryagiri begins its journey down the canals.

### 4. The Royal Road

- **Setting:** the eastern march on the border with Đại Long.
- **Introduces:**
  - the royal road, with **rest houses** that extend walkers' range and speed up caravans;
  - laterite walls and moats;
  - elephant militia;
  - **gifts and truces** with a rival city.
- **Threats:** 3 waves of Đại Long border fighting.
- **Goals:** survive the waves; bring relations with Đại Long to Neutral through gifts; Security 40.
- **Story:** the border war from Kambara's side. Sovanna rides out to make the truce, against the King's orders.

### 5. Suryagiri

- **Setting:** the outskirts of the capital, with a sacred site already laid out.
- **Introduces:**
  - a great monument in 6 stages: moat, causeway, outer gallery, terraces, the five towers, the reliefs and consecration;
  - sculptors' and stone carvers' guilds;
  - the dance hall.
- **Goals:** Suryagiri complete; Divine Favour 60.
- **Story:** the greatest work of the age is consecrated. Achar Vama dies soon after, his work done.

### 6. Tribute of the Sea

- **Setting:** a poor frontier on the coast facing Vijara.
- **Pressure:**
  - the usurper Lord Vrah Sena's court sends one heavy tribute request after another;
  - the starting budget is low;
  - storms hit the coast;
  - the only trade is by sea with Vijara and Hamsara.
- **Threats:** 2 pirate raids.
- **Goals:** fulfil 8 tribute requests; Prosperity 45; survive both raids.
- **Story:** Ja Kumar warns that Vijara's admiral is gathering a fleet. The court does not listen.

### 7. The Burning of Yasonagara

- **Setting:** a lakeside city in sight of the capital, during the flood season.
- **Pressure:**
  - a large naval invasion lands from the lake;
  - waves of refugees need housing quickly;
  - the capital's fall is scripted and can be seen on the horizon.
- **Goals:** survive 5 waves; house 1,500 refugees; keep the city's citadel intact.
- **Story:** the darkest mission of Campaign II.

### 8. The Exile's Return

- **Setting:** a new city on the royal road.
- **Introduces:**
  - royal hospitals;
  - **the shift in faith:** the Compassionate Path's patrons appear beside the Devas;
  - aid arriving as inbound grain from Đại Long, sent by old King Khai Sơn.
- **Goals:** health coverage over 80% of houses; population 3,000; Culture 45.
- **Story:** Jayadeva returns. *"Rebuild for the living."*

### 9. The Great City

- **Setting:** the ruins of Yasonagara.
- **Monument:** **the walls of the Great City**: the moat, the laterite walls and 5 face-carved gate towers, each one a stage.
- **Threats:** Vijara's last assault, 4 waves.
- **Goals:** walls and gates complete; survive all waves; Security 60.

### 10. The Temple of Four Faces

- **Setting:** the centre of the rebuilt Yasonagara.
- **Monument:** **the Temple of Four Faces**, in 5 stages.
- **Introduces:** a festival cycle, and peace with Vijara through relations.
- **Goals:** the temple complete; Divine Favour 75; Prosperity 60; relations with Vijara at Friendly.
- **Story:** forgiveness. The House of Ja's ships return as traders, and Campaign III unlocks.

## Campaign III — The Plain of Ten Thousand Temples (Tampadesa)

| # | Mission | Setting | WY | Type | New systems |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Golden River | A ruined village on the dry plain | 70–73 | M | Tampadesa buildings, drought, river-fed canals, monasteries, the Merit rating |
| 2 | The Granary Valley | The Kyautthin valley | 73–76 | M | Weirs on hill streams, river barges, rice requests |
| 3 | Fire and Clay | A kiln town | 76–80 | M | Brick works at scale, lacquerware, **the merit economy** |
| 4 | The Gilded Stupa | The Pugara riverside | 80–85 | H | The gilders' guild, the gold-leaf trade, a relic procession |
| 5 | The Hill Roads | Highland mines | 85–90 | M + S | Gem and jade mines, overland routes to both other realms, horse-archer militia, brick walls |
| 6 | The Port of the Sacred Goose | Hamsara | 90–96 | M | Two peoples with separate culture needs, the far traders, typhoons |
| 7 | The Temple of Infinite Wisdom | Pugara | 96–102 | H + M | A great monument built against treasury pressure |
| 8 | The Price of Merit | The central plain | 102–112 | M | Crisis: an empty treasury, tax-free temple lands, drought, unrest, policy choices |
| 9 | The Northern Storm | The northern frontier fortress | 120–124 | S | Siege forces with rams, cavalry, calling on allies |
| 10 | The Three Crowns | Pugara | 124–130 | S + H | The final siege, allied aid, the Plain of Ten Thousand Temples |

### 1. The Golden River

- **Setting:** a ruined village on the dry plain beside the Shwemyit.
- **Introduces:**
  - Tampadesa's buildings, and teak and brick housing;
  - **drought;**
  - canals fed from the river;
  - the monastery, which provides education and faith;
  - **Merit.**
- **Goals:** population 1,200; Merit 25; no famine for 2 years.
- **Story:** Thiha, a captive scholar, is given a ruin and told to prove his worth.

### 2. The Granary Valley

- **Setting:** an irrigated valley in the hills south of the capital.
- **Introduces:**
  - weirs on the hill streams;
  - branching canal networks;
  - river barges carrying rice to Pugara.
- **Goals:** fulfil 6 rice requests; population 2,000.
- **Story:** the valley becomes the realm's granary, and Mahathura takes notice.

### 3. Fire and Clay

- **Setting:** a town of kilns on the river.
- **Introduces:**
  - brick works at scale;
  - the lacquerware chain;
  - **the merit economy:** citizens donate to monasteries; elite houses build small **merit pagodas** on their own, which raise Merit; temple lands pay no tax.
- **Goals:** 1,000 bricks in storage; Merit 40; treasury 4,000. This first shows the tension between merit and treasury.

### 4. The Gilded Stupa

- **Setting:** the riverside at Pugara.
- **Monument:** **the Gilded Stupa**, in 4 stages: terraces, the bell, the spire, gilding and the relic chamber.
- **Introduces:** the gilders' guild, gold leaf traded in, and a relic procession festival.
- **Goals:** the stupa complete; Merit 55.
- **Story:** the King frees Thiha and makes him a governor.

### 5. The Hill Roads

- **Setting:** highland mining country.
- **Introduces:**
  - gem and jade mines;
  - overland routes to Đại Long and Kambara;
  - horse-archer militia;
  - brick walls.
- **Threats:** 3 raids by hill bandits.
- **Goals:** trade income 2,500 a year; survive the raids.

### 6. The Port of the Sacred Goose

- **Setting:** Hamsara, Thiha's homeland, now a province.
- **Introduces:**
  - **two peoples** with their own temples, festivals and satisfaction;
  - sea trade with the far traders;
  - typhoons.
- **Goals:** Prosperity 55; each people's satisfaction at least 60; sea exports worth 2,000 a year.
- **Story:** Thiha is seen as a collaborator at home and as a foreigner at court.

### 7. The Temple of Infinite Wisdom

- **Setting:** Pugara.
- **Monument:** **the Temple of Infinite Wisdom**, in 5 stages.
- **Pressure:** temple lands keep growing and the tax base shrinks.
- **Goals:** the temple complete; treasury 5,000 at the end.
- **Story:** Mahathura dies, and King Narathiri's temple frenzy begins.

### 8. The Price of Merit

- **Setting:** the central plain in crisis.
- **Pressure:**
  - the treasury starts empty;
  - much of the land is held tax-free by temples;
  - drought and famine;
  - unrest.
- **Introduces:** **policy choices**, such as returning land to the tax rolls, monastery-run granaries or royal donations, each with a trade-off between Merit and Prosperity.
- **Goals:** treasury 8,000; Merit at least 50; Security 50.
- **Story:** the moral heart of the campaign. Treasurer Maung Pe and Shin Dhammika speak for the two sides.

### 9. The Northern Storm

- **Setting:** the northern frontier fortress.
- **Threats:** the Steppe Khaganate's army, with cavalry raids and siege forces carrying rams, in 5 waves.
- **Introduces:** calling on allies: sending requests to Đại Long and Kambara, which answer according to relations and the routes already open.
- **Goals:** survive all waves with the fortress intact.

### 10. The Three Crowns

- **Setting:** Pugara at the end of an age.
- **Threats:** the Khaganate's final siege, through the wet season.
- **Aid:** Phan Minh's engineers build river stakes, and Lady Mealea's physicians raise health. Both depend on routes and relations.
- **Monument:** **the Plain of Ten Thousand Temples**, a district-scale monument completed by finishing a great temple and filling the plain with merit pagodas.
- **Goals:** survive until the end of WY 130; the monument complete; Merit 80.
- **Story:** Arslan Noyan withdraws, and the three steles end the game.

## Open questions

- **Number of missions:** 30 maps is a large content budget. Should some missions offer a choice of two maps, as in Pharaoh, or does each campaign have exactly 10?
- **Tutorials in later campaigns:** Campaign II and III each introduce new systems. Should their first missions have short tutorial prompts, or rely on advisor messages?
- **Mission length:** what is the target? About 1–2 hours each is typical of the reference games.
