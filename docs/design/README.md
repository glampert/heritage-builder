## Ground rules

- **No real names in the game.** Real civilisations, places and people are references only. All in-game names are fictional; see [world.md](world.md#rules-for-the-fiction).
- **The three realms** are Đại Long (ref. Đại Việt), Kambara (ref. the Khmer Empire) and Tampadesa (ref. Pagan/Bagan).
- **Numbers are placeholders** for balancing unless a document says otherwise.
- **Open questions** are listed at the end of each document. When one is answered, record the decision in that document and remove the question.
